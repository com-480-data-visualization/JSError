Our Methodology

Our data visualization platform combines multiple European social surveys and demographic studies to provide comprehensive insights into the social and economic landscape of Europe.

  • Data aggregation from multiple European surveys
  • Statistical analysis and trend identification
  • Interactive visualization development
  • Cross-country comparison capabilities
  • Real-time data exploration tools

Data Sources

Our dataset combines information from European Social Survey (ESS), Eurostat demographic data, Pew Research Center studies, and various national statistical offices across Europe. All data has been standardized and verified for accuracy and consistency.

Note: Data represents the most recent available information for each country and may vary in collection dates. All percentages are calculated based on valid responses excluding missing data.
